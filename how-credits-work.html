How credits work, and why we charge them

From the HelloTraveler team: we would love to make every part of this app free for everyone, forever. We built HelloTraveler because we believe every traveller deserves to know the story of the place in front of them.

The honest truth is that each answer costs us real money to produce. When you point your camera at a landmark, paid systems identify it, write its story, and speak that story aloud in your language. Behind that sit servers, storage, electricity, and the small team that keeps everything running smoothly while you travel. Credits are simply how we pass those real costs on, as fairly and simply as we can. There are no hidden charges: you always see the cost before you spend a credit.

What a credit buys you

  • Photo query (we identify the place in your photo and narrate its story): 1 credit
  • Discover Nearby, after your one free use: 1 credit per tap. Your results are saved to your journal, so you can open them again later for free.
  • Ask the Guide a follow-up question: free, with a fair daily limit
  • Replaying a narration you already received: free, it is saved for you
  • Our preloaded city journals (starting with Iloilo): free, always

Our promise

We priced credits to cover what the service actually costs to run, no more games than that. As we learn the true costs from real usage, we will revisit our pricing, and if the costs allow it, we will bring prices down. If anything about charges ever changes, we will say so here first, in plain language, before it takes effect.

Thank you for travelling with us.
